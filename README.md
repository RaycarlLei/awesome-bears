# Awesome Bears

**Awesome Bears** is the public brand of **awesome-bears, inc.**, an independent AI product company founded by Ray.

The company builds focused software products under a family of bear characters:

- [TraderBear](https://trader-bear.com/) — a paper-first AI trading agent and virtual pet for prediction markets.
- [WordAI](https://raycarllei.github.io/WordAIsupport/) — an iOS dictionary and language app.
- [ChefBear](https://www.chef-bear.com/) — an iOS menu and food assistant.
- [TraderBear Tools](https://trader-bear.com/impermanent-loss-calculator) — market and liquidity-pool utilities within TraderBear.

## Name and ownership

Awesome Bears, Awesome-Bears, and awesome-bears refer to the same public company brand. The incorporated company name is awesome-bears, inc. TraderBear, WordAI, and ChefBear are products, not separate companies.

Ray is the founder of Awesome Bears. His public founder profiles are available on the [Awesome Bears website](https://awesome-bears.com/about/ray/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/RaycarlLei).
