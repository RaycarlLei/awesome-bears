Awesome Bears

Awesome Bears is the public brand of awesome-bears, inc., an independent AI product company founded by Ray.

What Awesome Bears builds

The company creates focused software products under a family of bear characters. Each product has a distinct job and release status.

  • TraderBear is a paper-first AI trading agent and virtual pet for prediction markets.
  • WordAI is an iOS dictionary and language app.
  • ChefBear is an iOS menu and food assistant.
  • TraderBear Tools contains market and liquidity-pool utilities within TraderBear.

The name

Awesome Bears, Awesome-Bears, and awesome-bears refer to the same public company brand. The incorporated company name is awesome-bears, inc. TraderBear, WordAI, and ChefBear are products of the company, not separate companies.
